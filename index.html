Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

LitFit ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the LitFit mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use LitFit:

  • Account information: Name and email address when you sign in with Apple.
  • Workout data: Sessions you log, including muscles trained, duration, energy levels, and ratings.
  • Health & wellness data: Protein intake, water intake, sleep quality, and other self-reported metrics you choose to log.
  • Device information: Device type and OS version for app performance purposes.
  • Push notification tokens: Used to deliver workout reminders and recap notifications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the LitFit app experience.
  • To sync your workout data across your devices.
  • To send you workout reminders, streak alerts, and weekly/monthly recaps (only if you enable notifications).
  • To calculate personal records and performance trends.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), hosted in North America. Your workout data is private and is only accessible by you — we do not share, sell, or monetize your personal data.

Third-Party Services

LitFit uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and push notifications.
  • Sign in with Apple: Account creation and authentication.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen within the app.

Children's Privacy

LitFit is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
